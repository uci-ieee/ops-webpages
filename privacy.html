Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026. Applies to the OPS Submissions application used by students enrolled in Open Project Space.

Open Project Space (OPS) is a program run by the student branch of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) at UC Irvine. OPS Submissions is the application enrolled students use to sign in and turn in their project work. This page explains what that application collects, who can see it, and how to have it removed.

OPS Submissions is operated by student officers of IEEE at UC Irvine. It is not an official University of California, Irvine service, and it is not the university’s system of record for coursework or grades.

Signing in

Sign-in uses your UCI Google account. Only addresses ending in @uci.edu that already appear on the OPS roster can sign in.

We never create or store a password for you. There is no OPS password to choose, remember, or reset. Your password is handled entirely by Google and is never sent to us.

When you sign in, Google tells us only three things: your email address, your name as it appears on your Google account, and a unique sign-in identifier. We request only the standard openid, profile, and email permissions. We have no access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service, and we cannot act on your account.

What we store

  • Your UCInetID, taken from the first part of your UCI email address
  • Your email address and name, as provided by Google
  • Your role in the program (student, instructor, or administrator)
  • The lab section you are enrolled in
  • The date you last signed in
  • The project work you submit, once file submission is available in the application, along with the date and time you submitted it

We do not collect your student ID number, date of birth, address, phone number, or payment information, and we do not ask you for them.

Why we store it

Solely to run the program: to confirm you are an enrolled OPS student, to show your instructor the work you turned in, and to let officers see who still needs help. We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to build a profile of you.

Who can see your information

  • You can see your own section and your own submissions.
  • Your instructor can see the roster and submitted work for the lab sections they are assigned to, and no others.
  • OPS administrators — a small number of student officers — can see all sections, all rosters, and all submitted work.

We do not disclose your information to anyone outside the program, except where the university or the law requires it.

Services we rely on

Running the application means a few other companies process this data on our behalf:

  • Google — sign-in
  • Neon — database hosting, in the United States
  • Vercel — application hosting

Connections between your browser, the application, and its database are encrypted.

How long we keep it

Roster information and submitted work are kept for the academic year they belong to, so that instructors can review work and resolve grading questions. Afterwards they may be retained for program records or deleted at the discretion of the officers.

Removing your information

You can ask us to delete your account and your submitted work at any time by emailing the address below from your UCI account. Note that if you are enrolled in the course, deleting your submissions may leave you without proof that you completed the work, so talk to your instructor first.

You can also disconnect OPS Submissions from your Google account at any time through the security settings of your Google account. Doing so stops you from signing in; it does not by itself delete work you have already submitted.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
